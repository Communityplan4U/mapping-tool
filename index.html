Community Land Use Tool

Help decide how public land should be used in your neighbourhood.

This map shows public land sites where your neighbourhood is collecting ideas. Pick a site to rank the uses you'd most like to see there, leave a comment, and support ideas other residents have already proposed. You can also use the 📍 Add a feedback marker button on the map, then click any spot, to leave feedback about retail, housing, transportation, public realm, parks, or heritage there.

Map layers

Open data from the City of Toronto. Turn a layer on to see it on the map; click a shape for details.

    Community feedback

    Feedback residents have left by clicking the map, grouped by address — anyone can add another comment at a location others have already commented on, and vote on comments. Turn a topic on or off to show or hide its pins.
