            How this works

            Two ways to leave feedback

            Click any shape or pin on the map — a school, park, vacant lot, or existing idea/archive entry — to leave feedback about that specific place. To leave feedback somewhere without a shape, use the Add feedback marker button, then click the map where you'd like to add one.

            Ranking uses & categories

            Map layers and community feedback are organized around the same categories, based on the Little Jamaica Community Development Action Plan's focus areas. Where public land sites are listed, you can also rank the uses you'd most like to see there and support ideas other residents have already proposed.

            What happens to your idea/archive entry

            Idea/archive entries and rankings post immediately and are visible to everyone — there's no review queue before that happens. Other residents can vote on entries and rank site uses too; look for the tally next to each one.

            Moderation

            Submissions are anonymous and public — there's no login, just a private token in your browser so you can toggle your own votes. Adding your name or contact info when adding an idea/archive entry is always optional; your name is shown alongside it if you add one, and your contact info is only used by neighbourhood organizers to follow up, never shown publicly. Nothing is reviewed before it posts; neighbourhood organizers remove anything inappropriate (personal attacks, hate speech, spam) after the fact. See something that should come down? Contact your neighbourhood organizers.
